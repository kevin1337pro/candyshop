# FORME — Originale Bildproduktion

Alle drei Bilder wurden mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug (nicht mit der API/CLI) eigens für diese Kleidungsshop-Demo generiert. Keine realen Produkte oder Marken werden abgebildet. Die Bilder und Beispieltexte müssen vor realen Verkäufen mit den tatsächlich angebotenen Kleidungsstücken übereinstimmen.

Dateien: `public/images/hero.png`, `public/images/products.png`, `public/images/editorial.png`. Im WordPress-Theme sind Kopien unter `wordpress/forme/assets/` enthalten.

## Hero-Prompt
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium clothing ecommerce hero for FORME. Wide 3:2 high-end fashion editorial photograph of two adult models in an industrial concrete urban setting. Woman in a black tailored oversized blazer over an ivory top; man in a textured dark chocolate zip jacket with stone trousers. Landscape full frame, natural candid standing poses, both subjects middle-right, composition appropriate to fill the right half of a clothing website hero. Sculptural afternoon sunlight, sophisticated desaturated film palette, premium European fashion magazine look. Honest skin and rich apparel texture. No text, no logos, no watermark.

## Produkt-Prompt
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium clothing ecommerce product catalog contact sheet. A square 2x2 contact sheet with four equal fully separate quadrants, NO borders and NO text. Top left: isolated heavyweight ivory oversized T-shirt. Top right: isolated black boxy zip jacket. Bottom left: isolated straight wide-leg dark blue jeans. Bottom right: isolated dark brown ribbed knit sweater. Uniform pale cool gray studio background. Each garment front view, suspended ghost mannequin presentation, centered with substantial margin and fully inside its own quadrant. Equal-sized quadrants must be cleanly usable as four independent CSS background crops. Premium editorial studio product photography with honest apparel texture. Four garments only, one per quadrant. No people, no hangers, no borders, no text, no logos, no watermark.

## Editorial-Prompt
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait editorial photo for a premium clothing ecommerce story section. Portrait 3:4 high-end editorial photograph of an adult woman wearing an oversized dark gray blazer over a white T-shirt and loose trousers, seated on a concrete block against a raw architectural wall. Portrait, fashion editorial composition, natural seated pose. Natural side light, premium magazine look. Honest skin, visible fabric texture, raw concrete. No text, no logos, no watermark.
